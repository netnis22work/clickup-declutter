# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)
- First release.
- Hide rules for AI entry points (AI rail, Brain², AI Chats, Skills, Artifacts, AI automation buttons).
- Hide rules for Chat (Chat rail, Channels, Direct Messages, Replies, Posts, "Add Channel" chip).
- Hide rules for unused rail items (Planner, Teams, Whiteboards, Forms, Clips, Install, Upgrade) and top-bar icons (SyncUp, record clip, talk to text, notepad).
- Hides "What's new" cards, product tours and upgrade banners.
- Popup in ClickUp's own design language: collapsible groups, per-item and per-group switches, search, a live "Hiding N items" count, a master switch and a two-step Reset.
- The popup follows your ClickUp look: light/dark mode and your workspace theme color are read from ClickUp's design tokens.
- Flat icon and store art in ClickUp violet, with no gradients.
- "Pick element" to hide anything else, with parent/child navigation.
